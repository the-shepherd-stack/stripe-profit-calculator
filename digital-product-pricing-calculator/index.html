What this scenario already includes

Pricing pressure from multiple directions

The preset assumes a $100 list price, $5 delivery cost, a 10% coupon, 20% VAT, 30% affiliate payout, 5% refund reserve, and standard 2.9% + $0.30 Stripe fees.

What number to trust first

Net profit and break-even list price tell you whether the offer survives real selling conditions. If those numbers look weak, your launch plan probably needs a higher price, a smaller discount, or a lower partner cut.
